Earth Science Key Concept

Gravity is constantly pulling soil down slopes. When water saturates soil, it adds heavy weight and reduces friction, triggering slumps along curved failure surfaces.

Tip: Increase rain slider to see failure! Part 1 of 7

What causes slope failure?

1. Over-steepening: Erosion or road cutting makes slopes too steep.
2. Water Saturation: Rain fills pore spaces, adding weight and lubricating rock boundaries.
3. Earthquakes: Seismic shaking triggers sudden loss of cohesion.
